# PDF Scan privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-30.*

PDF Scan is a document scanner for students. It works without an account, and your documents
stay on your phone.

## What never leaves your phone
- Your scans, photos, PDFs and other documents.
- The text the app reads from them (OCR). Text recognition runs on the phone.
- Your courses, document names, profile (name, roll, section, institution), notes,
  annotations and signatures.

These leave the phone only when **you** send them: sharing a file, saving it to a folder,
or making a backup and putting it somewhere.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
PDF Scan is free. It may show a small banner on the Home and Files screens. You can choose
to watch a video ad to get Pro features for 1 hour (a few times a day), and some Pro tasks,
such as converting a file, ask you to watch one video ad first. There are no ads while you
scan, edit, save, submit or read, and no full-screen ads that you didn't ask for.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and count ads, Google receives your phone's
**advertising ID**, basic device information (model, operating system, language), your
approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads (views and
taps). Google does not receive your documents or their text. See Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Turning off personalised ads:**
- In the app: Me → Privacy & security → **Personalised ads**. Ads still show, but are not based on
  your advertising ID or interests.
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with
  Google's form first, and you can change your answer later.
- On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings
  (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

## App settings (Firebase Remote Config)
The app downloads a small set of settings from Google Firebase (for example, whether ads are
on). To do this, Firebase receives an installation ID made for this app and basic device
information. Nothing about your documents is sent.

## Optional: usage counts (Firebase Analytics)
**Off by default.** If you turn on Me → Privacy & security → **Help improve PDF Scan**, the app
sends anonymous counts of six things only: the app was opened, a scan was completed (with its
number of pages), a document was saved, a document was submitted, a Pro pass was started,
and a backup was made. These never include names, course names, file names or any text. Turn
it off at any time; turning it off also clears the anonymous app id Analytics kept.

## Optional: crash reports (Sentry)
**Off by default.** If you turn on Me → Privacy & security → **Send anonymous crash reports**, the
app sends technical details when it crashes (where in the code it failed, phone model,
operating system). File paths, document names and scanned text are removed before sending.

## Children
PDF Scan is meant for students aged 13 and over.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Your advertising ID can be
reset in the phone's settings. To ask about data held by the optional services above,
contact us.
